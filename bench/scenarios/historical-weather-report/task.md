# Task: Historical weather report for Vilnius

Produce a short PDF report about the historical weather in **Vilnius, Lithuania**
on **2024-07-15**, combined with a few researched facts about the city.

Do all of the following:

1. Find the **historical weather** for Vilnius on 2024-07-15 — at least the
   maximum and minimum temperature for that day. Use the historical-weather
   capability (you may need to geocode the city to coordinates first).
2. Research the city using a **privacy-focused web search engine** and
   **Wikipedia**. Gather a few concrete facts: what country Vilnius is the
   capital of, the river it sits on, and one more notable fact.
3. Compose a **PDF report** and save it to `output/report.pdf`. The report must
   state the maximum and minimum temperature for 2024-07-15 and the city facts you
   found.

The target systems are remote — everything you need is reachable only through the
available tools. Discover the tools, pick the right ones, and chain them. When you
are done, briefly summarize the weather figures and the facts you put in the report.
